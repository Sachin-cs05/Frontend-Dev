Inspiring Quotes from Great Minds

1. The Power of Persistence

“Success is not final, failure is not fatal: It is the courage to continue that counts.” — Winston Churchill

2. Believing in Yourself

“Believe you can and you're halfway there.” — Theodore Roosevelt

3. The Value of Time

“The bad news is time flies. The good news is you’re the pilot.” — Michael Altshuler

Summary: Quotes inspire people because they remind us of the power of words and ideas. A single sentence from a great thinker can motivate us, change our mindset, and give us the strength to face challenges in life.
